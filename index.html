Mockingbird Backups

A personal backup connection for copying selected local archives to the account owner's Google Drive and verifying that they can be downloaded intact.

The desktop utility uses rclone and a dedicated Google OAuth client. It requests the limited drive.file permission to work with files it creates or files explicitly made available to the app. It is intended for the owner's private use and is not a public file-storage service.

The information on this website is public. Backup contents, Google credentials, and the owner's development files are not published here.
